# Carousel Architecture Frameworks

### Framework 1: The Contrarian Teardown
- Slide 1: Why 90% of [Niche] are doing [Topic] completely wrong.
- Slide 2: The Old Way vs The Hidden Cost (Frustration & wasted time).
- Slide 3: The Counterintuitive Shift (The new mechanism).
- Slide 4: Real-World Case Study / Concrete Example.
- Slide 5: The 3-Step Action Checklist.
- Slide 6: Summary & Call-To-Action ("Save this cheat sheet for your next sprint").

### Framework 2: The Step-by-Step SOP
- Slide 1: How to [High-Value Outcome] without [Major Friction/Cost].
- Slide 2: Prerequisite Setup & Mindset.
- Slide 3: Phase 1 (Foundation / Input).
- Slide 4: Phase 2 (Execution / Core Action).
- Slide 5: Phase 3 (Review & QA).
- Slide 6: Final Takeaway & CTA.

### Framework 3: The 4-Part Framework Teardown
- Slide 1: The 4-Part Engine Behind [Winning Example].
- Slide 2: Pillar 1 (Hook / Angle).
- Slide 3: Pillar 2 (Mechanism / Proof).
- Slide 4: Pillar 3 (Offer / Urgency).
- Slide 5: Pillar 4 (CTA / Friction Removal).
- Slide 6: Actionable Summary + Comment prompt.
