# Optics & Focal Length Reference

### 1. 100mm f/2.8 Macro Lens
- Razor-sharp focus on texture, material embossing, micro water droplets, glass bevels.
- Zero barrel distortion, ideal for tight product labeling and craftsmanship details.

### 2. 85mm f/1.4 Prime Lens
- Shallow depth of field, creamy background bokeh, tack-sharp subject separation.
- The standard for hero product portraits and pedestal staging.

### 3. 35mm / 50mm f/1.8 Natural Lens
- Natural human eye perspective, rich environmental context.
- Ideal for lifestyle scenes with hands, desks, kitchen counters, or vanity tables.

### 4. 24mm Dynamic Wide Angle
- Low-angle Dutch angle perspective, dramatic scale and dynamic energy.
- Ideal for athletic gear, outdoor footwear, and high-impact hero angles.
