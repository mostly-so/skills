# Studio Lighting & Light Shaping Reference

### 1. Commercial Clean Studio
- Overhead diffusion softbox at 45 degrees, subtle warm rim/edge light, clean white frosted fill reflector.
- Best for: Luxury packaging, glass bottles, cosmetics, tech hardware.

### 2. Warm Lifestyle / Golden Hour
- Warm natural golden hour morning sunlight streaming through sheer linen curtains, soft atmospheric dust motes.
- Best for: Wellness, skincare, morning routines, lifestyle apparel.

### 3. Dramatic Premium Luxury
- Moody chiaroscuro, focused spotlighting, high-contrast dark tones, subtle rim highlights separating subject from deep background.
- Best for: Spirits, luxury watches, premium fragrances, gaming gear.

### 4. High-Key Modern Tech
- Bright, clean, shadowless diffuse lighting, crisp neutral white balance (5500K).
- Best for: Clean software devices, modern minimalist accessories.
